- One primary sample-roast CTA, early-access secondary action, a separate creator invitation and concise benefit copy.
- Mobile form starts around 370px down the page; its one-row submit is around 626px at 390×844 (previously 1,420px and 3,418px).

## Validation and operational limits

Regression tests exercise duplicate-lot invariance, score consistency, provenance, missing-original rejection, PostgreSQL RLS, explicit original review, rollback, payment matching/replay and delivery claims. Type checks, production build, production dependency audit and hosted checks are required before merge.

Live payment execution and emails are not used as tests. Razorpay/email provider configuration and lifecycle work remain separate operational requirements; this release does not enable recurring billing or broker authorisation. Original review remains a human responsibility. Source estimates are not transformed into audited performance by this release. Development-only Tailwind dependency advisories remain outside the production audit.
